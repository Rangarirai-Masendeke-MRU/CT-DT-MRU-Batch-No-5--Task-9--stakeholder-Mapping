### Project Topic: Smart City with all vehicles as E-Vehicles
This task identifies the key individuals, groups, and organizations that have an interest in or are impacted by the transition of an entire city's fleet to electric mobility.

### Stakeholder Analysis:
* **Primary Stakeholders:** Citizens, city planners, and transit authorities who are directly involved in daily operations.
* **Secondary Stakeholders:** Energy providers and EV manufacturers who provide the necessary infrastructure and hardware.
* **Tertiary Stakeholders:** Regulatory bodies and environmental advocates who influence policy and long-term sustainability goals.
